# Workshop 1: writing, readers, and tools

**Presentation draft 0.5 · 9 October 2026**

Valerie Sutton and Steve Slevinski present a conversation about writing signed
languages, developing orthography, and the tools that preserve and connect that
work. The session includes a SignMaker demonstration and audience discussion.

Workshop 1 is scheduled for **10 October 2026**, hosted online by the Wiki Hour
Initiative. The organizers will provide the time and joining details. They have
arranged International Sign interpretation, live captions, a moderator, and recording.
They plan to archive the session on YouTube and Wikimedia Commons.

## Proposed programme

The current programme allocates forty minutes to the presentation and twenty to
audience questions. Val contributes personal and historical perspectives; Steve
explains the writing model, current software, and possible Wikimedia integration.

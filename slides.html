06 · Two compatible encodings

Two encodings.
The same composition.

FSW

Formal SignWriting in ASCII
The canonical encoding string for spelling a sign.

SWU

SignWriting in Unicode
An experimental encoding design, 100% compatible with FSW.

SWU is not part of the Unicode standard.
Both FSW and SWU are incompatible with the official SignWriting design introduced in Unicode 8.
